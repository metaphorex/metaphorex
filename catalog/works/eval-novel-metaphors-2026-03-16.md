Evaluation set of novel conceptual metaphors generated to test whether
frontier models can produce structurally original mappings rather than
reproducing known metaphors from the literature. Covers software
architecture, organizational dynamics, and distributed systems domains
with source frames drawn from biology, governance, urban planning,
forensics, and theater.
