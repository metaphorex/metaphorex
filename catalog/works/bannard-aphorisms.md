Survey of aphorisms about art, craft, and creative practice -- many
attributed to or collected by Walter Darby Bannard -- that have migrated
into design, product management, and software engineering as conceptual
metaphors, heuristics, and paradigms. Includes studio wisdom about
editing, quality, and the nature of creative judgment.
