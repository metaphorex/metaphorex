Survey of metaphors from developmental psychology that structure theory
and practice in education, therapy, and management. Covers Winnicott
(holding environment, transitional object), Bowlby (secure base,
attachment), Bruner (scaffolding), Vygotsky (ZPD), and Erikson (stages).
