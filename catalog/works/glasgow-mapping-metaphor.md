A database of metaphorical connections between semantic domains in English,
built from the Historical Thesaurus of English at the University of Glasgow.
Maps systematic links between ~400 semantic categories, documenting how
vocabulary transfers between domains across the full history of English from
Old English to the present. Provides historical depth that complements the
synchronic analysis of Lakoff and Johnson's conceptual metaphor theory.
