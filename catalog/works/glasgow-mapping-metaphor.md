A database of metaphorical connections across the full history of English,
built on the Historical Thesaurus of English at the University of Glasgow.
The project systematically maps semantic transfers between conceptual
domains as attested in the historical lexical record, providing empirical
evidence for metaphorical mappings that cognitive linguistics has
identified through analysis of contemporary usage. Contains ~14,000
metaphorical connections across ~400 semantic categories.
