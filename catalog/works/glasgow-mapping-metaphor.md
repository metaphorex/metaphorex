A database of metaphorical connections across the full history of English,
built on the Historical Thesaurus of English at the University of Glasgow.
Maps semantic domains to each other based on lexical evidence of metaphorical
transfer, covering Old English through the present. Unlike the Master
Metaphor List, which draws on cognitive linguistics theory, this resource
is grounded in historical lexicography -- it identifies metaphors through
the diachronic record of word senses rather than through introspective
analysis.
