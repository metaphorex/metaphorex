130 numbered lessons on theatrical directing, each a compressed aphorism
with commentary. Encodes practical wisdom about leading creative work:
giving feedback without prescribing, creating conditions rather than
results, managing ensemble dynamics. Supplemented by Stanislavski,
Johnstone, and Bogart.
