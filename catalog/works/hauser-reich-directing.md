Frank Hauser's 130 practical notes on the craft of directing for stage and
screen, edited and expanded by Russell Reich. A source of mental models and
metaphors for leadership, feedback, and the management of creative work.
The notes encode decades of directing experience as concise, transferable
principles -- many of which apply far beyond theater.
