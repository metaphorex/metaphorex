Composite source combining W. Edwards Deming's 14 Points for Management
(from *Out of the Crisis*, 1986) and the Toyota Production System glossary
developed by Taiichi Ohno and Shigeo Shingo. These sources share a common
intellectual lineage (Shewhart -> Deming -> Ohno/Shingo -> TPS) and their
concepts cross-reference heavily.
