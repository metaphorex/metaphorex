Survey of comedy writing and improvisational theater terminology that has
crossed into organizational, therapeutic, and educational discourse. Includes
improv principles (yes-and, offers and blocks), writers' room jargon
(laying pipe, hat on a hat), and performance concepts (callback, lampshading).
