Richard Gabriel's essay arguing that software systems prioritizing
implementation simplicity over interface correctness spread faster and
ultimately win. Originally a section of "Lisp: Good News, Bad News, How to
Win Big." Contrasts the "New Jersey approach" (worse-is-better) with the
"MIT approach" (the-right-thing).
