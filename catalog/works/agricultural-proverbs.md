Collection of proverbs, idioms, and folk expressions rooted in
agricultural practice -- planting, cultivation, harvest, animal
husbandry, and land management. These encode pre-industrial wisdom
about effort, timing, consequence, and difficulty that has migrated
into general language as dead or dying metaphors.
