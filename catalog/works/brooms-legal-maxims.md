Herbert Broom's collection of Latin legal maxims classified and illustrated
with cases from English common law. First published in 1845, it went through
ten editions (the last in 1939) and remains a standard reference for the
foundational principles of Anglo-American jurisprudence. Each maxim is
presented with its Latin form, English translation, and extensive case law
illustrating its application and limits.
