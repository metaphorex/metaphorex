Herbert Broom's compendium of Latin legal maxims with English
commentary, first published in 1845. The standard Victorian reference
for common-law maxims, covering principles of evidence, property,
obligation, and statutory interpretation. Many maxims have escaped
their legal context to become folk wisdom or cognitive heuristics.
