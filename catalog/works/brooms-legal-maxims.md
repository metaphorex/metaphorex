A foundational collection of Latin legal maxims classified and illustrated
with cases from English common law. First published in 1845, it became a
standard reference in Anglo-American legal education. The maxims encode
structural principles about justice, evidence, property, and governance
that have migrated far beyond their legal origins into everyday reasoning.
