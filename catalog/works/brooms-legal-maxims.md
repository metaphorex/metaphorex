A treatise collecting, classifying, and explicating the foundational maxims
of English common law. First published in 1845, the work went through ten
editions (the last in 1939) and became a standard reference for legal
education in common-law jurisdictions. Broom organized maxims by topic --
statutory construction, property, procedure, evidence -- and provided
extensive case-law illustrations for each. The maxims are not merely
rules but compressed structural principles that reveal how the common-law
system reasons about rights, obligations, and the limits of legal authority.
