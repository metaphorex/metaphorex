Survey of aphorisms and heuristics from surgery and clinical medicine that
function as conceptual metaphors in broader decision-making discourse. Covers
triage, differential diagnosis, first do no harm, and other medical wisdom
that migrated from the operating room and battlefield hospital into business,
software, and everyday language.
