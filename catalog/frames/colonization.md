Establishing control over new territory by settling it, extracting its
resources, and imposing the colonizer's governance structures. The colonization
frame provides roles for expansion (the colonizer), resistance (indigenous
populations), extraction (resources flowing from periphery to center), and
administration (governance at a distance). It is structurally productive but
politically charged: any metaphor that imports colonization imports its history
of exploitation, displacement, and unequal power relations.
