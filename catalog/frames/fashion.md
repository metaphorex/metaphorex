The cycle of styles that come into vogue, spread through social imitation,
and fall out of favor -- clothing, but also hair, architecture, language,
and ideas. Fashion is distinct from textiles (the material craft) because
it foregrounds the social dynamics of adoption and abandonment: what is
"in" and "out," who sets trends and who follows them, and the relentless
temporality of seasons and collections. As a source domain, fashion maps
naturally onto any target where beliefs, practices, or preferences cycle
through popularity without necessarily improving.
