The cognitive operation of moving from concrete particulars to general
categories, and the resulting hierarchy of levels from specific to
universal. Distinguished from software abstraction (its most prominent
application domain) by its generality across philosophy, linguistics,
mathematics, and everyday reasoning. Powers metaphors about ladders
of abstraction, layers, and levels.
