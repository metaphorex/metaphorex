The systematic investigation of past events through physical and
documentary evidence. The forensic frame is distinguished from
surveillance (which watches in real time) by its temporal orientation:
forensics works backward from traces to reconstruct what happened.
The investigator does not observe the event but infers it from the
evidence it left behind. This frame maps onto incident response,
audit analysis, and any domain where understanding requires
reconstructing a causal sequence from fragmentary after-the-fact records.
