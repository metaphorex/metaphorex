Structured and unstructured ludic activity. As a source domain, games and play
contribute the logic of rules, turns, strategy, opponents, and stakes. Distinct
from the puzzles-and-games frame (which emphasizes cognitive challenge) in that
it foregrounds the embodied, social, and competitive dimensions -- the rope you
pull, the ball you throw, the person across from you.
