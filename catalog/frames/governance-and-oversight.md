The domain of directing and monitoring the exercise of power. Distinguished
from governance alone by the emphasis on oversight mechanisms -- the structures
that watch the watchers, verify compliance, and hold authority accountable.
Encompasses audit, review, separation of powers, and the recursive problem of
who oversees the overseers.
