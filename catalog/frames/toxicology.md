The study of poisons, doses, and their effects on organisms. Central
insight: the dose makes the poison. Small quantities of contamination
can produce outsized effects, and harm depends on the pathway of
exposure. A potent source domain for data corruption, cache poisoning,
and adversarial input attacks where tiny malicious inputs produce
catastrophic outputs.
