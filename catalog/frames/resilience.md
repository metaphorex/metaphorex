The capacity of a system, organism, or structure to absorb disturbance and
reorganize while retaining essential function. As a source domain, resilience
supplies vocabulary of shocks and stressors, recovery time, adaptive capacity,
brittleness thresholds, and reserves. It draws on ecology (Holling's adaptive
cycle), materials science (elastic deformation), and psychology (trauma
recovery). When we say an organization is "resilient," that a system "bounced
back," or that a design is "brittle," we are drawing on this frame.
