The practice of finding one's way through physical space: following trails,
leaving markers, reading landmarks, backtracking when lost. Navigation
presupposes that space is large enough to get lost in and that the cost
of being lost is high enough to justify leaving traces. The frame maps
productively onto any domain where users move through information and
need to know where they are and how to get back.
