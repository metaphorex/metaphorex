The domain of reasoning about the dangers, unintended consequences, and
ethical hazards of technological innovation. Encompasses risks from AI,
biotechnology, nuclear energy, genetic engineering, and other fields where
human creation may exceed human control. Productive as a target domain
because technology risk is abstract and probabilistic -- difficult to reason
about without concrete frames -- which is why science fiction metaphors
(Frankenstein, Skynet, grey goo) dominate public discourse about it. The
structural challenge is that real technology risks are typically systemic,
gradual, and distributed, while the metaphors borrowed to describe them
tend to be dramatic, sudden, and personified.
