The domain of threats, vulnerabilities, and failure modes in technological
systems. Encompasses single points of failure, attack surfaces, cascading
breakdowns, and the gap between a system's intended behavior and its actual
behavior under stress. As a target domain it attracts metaphors from warfare,
mythology, and medicine because technological failures are often described
in terms of wounds, invasions, and hidden weaknesses -- language that makes
abstract system properties emotionally legible.
