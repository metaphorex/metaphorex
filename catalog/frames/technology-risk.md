The domain of threats, trade-offs, and unintended consequences arising from
technological change. Covers the tension between innovation and preservation,
the risks of adoption and the costs of resistance, and the ways that new
technologies redistribute power, reshape environments, and create
vulnerabilities that did not previously exist. As a target domain it draws
on spatial metaphors (frontiers, borders), biological metaphors (infection,
mutation), and moral metaphors (corruption, innocence) to make abstract
technological dynamics concrete and emotionally legible.
