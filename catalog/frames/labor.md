The expenditure of human effort to accomplish tasks, especially under
conditions of obligation or compulsion. As a source domain, labor imports
questions of who works, who benefits, and what work costs the worker.
The frame foregrounds the tension between the instrumental value of work
(it produces something) and its human cost (it consumes time, energy,
and autonomy). When technologies are framed through labor metaphors --
as workers, servants, or slaves -- the framing imports assumptions about
obedience, replaceability, and the moral status of the entity performing
the work.
