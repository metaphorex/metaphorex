Movement of objects or substances from one location to another. As a source
domain, spatial motion provides metaphors for emotional displacement, the
relocation of qualities from one entity to another, and the idea that
psychological properties can travel between persons. The frame encodes the
structural insight that what is moved retains its identity even in a new
location, and that the act of transfer implies both a source that loses and
a recipient that gains.
