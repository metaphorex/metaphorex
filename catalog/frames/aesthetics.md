The perception and judgment of beauty, ugliness, and artistic quality.
Encompasses both sensory pleasure and cultivated taste. As a target domain,
aesthetics draws heavily on nature (flowers, light, seasons) and on bodily
sensation (sweetness, smoothness). As a source domain, aesthetic vocabulary
lends evaluative weight to non-artistic contexts: an "elegant" proof, an
"ugly" hack.
