The ways individuals define themselves and are defined by others through
group membership, social categories, and relational positioning. Includes
the mechanisms of inclusion and exclusion, the costs of nonconformity,
and the formation of identity through contrast with out-groups. A target
domain for metaphors from animal husbandry (black sheep, pecking order),
theater (playing a role), and spatial metaphors (insider/outsider,
marginal).
