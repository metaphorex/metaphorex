Systematic inquiry into fundamental questions about reality, knowledge,
and value. As a target domain, philosophy receives metaphors from
virtually every source frame -- war (attacking arguments), architecture
(building systems), vision (seeing the truth). As a source frame, it
exports structural distinctions (essential vs. accidental, form vs.
matter) that organize thinking in other fields. Its roles (argument,
premise, category) provide the vocabulary for reasoning about reasoning.
