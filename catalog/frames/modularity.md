The design principle of constructing systems from discrete, interchangeable
units with well-defined boundaries. As a target domain, modularity borrows
from carpentry (knock-down joints, panels), biology (organs, cells), and
construction (prefabrication, standardized components). The domain encodes
the fundamental trade-off between the tight integration that maximizes
performance and the loose coupling that maximizes flexibility.
