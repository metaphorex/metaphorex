The cognitive activity of assessing worth, quality, or fitness --
sorting good from bad, ranking alternatives, and reaching verdicts.
Evaluation draws heavily on physical metaphors of separation (wheat
from chaff, gold from dross), measurement (weighing evidence), and
spatial ranking (above/below a bar). As a target domain it absorbs
source frames from agriculture, metallurgy, and law.
