Narratives centered on transgression, the uncanny, and the consequences of
overreaching ambition -- typically involving a creator or protagonist who
violates natural or moral boundaries and faces horrific consequences. As a
source domain, Gothic fiction provides the vocabulary for talking about
creation gone wrong: the thing that was made to serve turns monstrous, the
boundary that was crossed cannot be uncrossed, the creator is destroyed by
the creation. Structurally characterized by the inversion of intention
(benign aim produces horrific outcome) and the return of the repressed
(what was hidden or denied resurfaces in monstrous form).
