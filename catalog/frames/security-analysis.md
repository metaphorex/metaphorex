The practice of modeling, measuring, and mitigating threats to systems.
Security analysis creates a vocabulary for reasoning about risk that
borrows heavily from military, medical, and spatial metaphors. It is
both a target domain (receiving metaphors from war, toxicology,
architecture) and a source domain (exporting "attack surface" and
"blast radius" into business and organizational risk management).
