The domain of binding agreements, legal obligations, and the gap between
written terms and actual expectations. Contract law distinguishes between
explicit terms (what the document says) and implied terms (what reasonable
parties would expect). This distinction maps powerfully onto API design,
organizational promises, and any system where a documented interface
diverges from actual behavior.
