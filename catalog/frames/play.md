Children's play and recreational activity: bounded spaces where actions
have reduced consequences, rules can be invented and discarded, and
experimentation is the point. The sandbox, the playground, the toy --
all share the quality of being safe simulations of real-world activity.
Play is how organisms learn without paying the full cost of failure.
