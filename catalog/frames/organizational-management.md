The domain of hierarchical organizations, their structures, and the
dynamics of advancement within them. Encompasses promotion, competence
assessment, role assignment, and the pathologies that emerge when
organizational incentives misalign with individual capability. As a
source domain, it exports metaphors of hierarchy, promotion beyond
capacity, and institutional dysfunction to technology and other fields.
