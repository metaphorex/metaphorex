The domain of groups attempting to produce shared goods through individual
contributions. Collective action foregrounds the tension between individual
rationality (why contribute when you can benefit without contributing?) and
group rationality (everyone benefits if everyone contributes). The frame
encodes problems of coordination, monitoring, and enforcement that arise
whenever outcomes depend on aggregate behavior rather than individual effort.
