The techniques and structural principles of constructing comedy. As a source
domain, comedy craft provides precise vocabulary for misdirection, timing,
structural dependency, and audience expectation management. The frame
foregrounds the engineering of surprise -- comedy works by building an
expectation and then violating it in a way that produces pleasure rather than
confusion. Its metaphorical transfers are strongest when the target domain
involves information sequencing, structural dependencies, or the management
of attention.
