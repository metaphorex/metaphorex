The systematic process of empirical investigation through observation,
hypothesis formation, experimentation, and revision. As a source domain,
the scientific method foregrounds falsifiability, controlled experiment,
reproducibility, and the provisional nature of all conclusions. Its
metaphorical extensions tend to import the prestige of scientific
certainty while often discarding the actual rigor.
