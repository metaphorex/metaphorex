The domain of thermal energy -- temperature, combustion, conduction, and
radiation. Heat transfers from hotter to cooler bodies, accumulates in
enclosed spaces, and produces visible effects (glow, steam, warping).
As a source domain, heat maps intensity onto temperature, control onto
containment, and danger onto combustion. The embodied grounding is
immediate: everyone knows what it feels like to be too hot, to touch
something burning, to feel warmth spread through the body.
