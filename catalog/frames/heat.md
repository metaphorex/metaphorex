Thermal sensation and temperature change -- the embodied experience of
hot, warm, cool, and cold. As a source domain, heat maps onto emotional
intensity (burning rage, warm affection, cold indifference), social
relations (heated debate, warm welcome), and cognitive states (hot take,
cool reasoning). The mapping is grounded in physiology: strong emotions
literally raise body temperature.
