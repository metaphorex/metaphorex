The craft of installing and maintaining systems that convey fluids through
enclosed channels. As a source domain, plumbing foregrounds hidden
infrastructure that is noticed only when it fails, the difference between
flow and blockage, the importance of joints and seals at connection points,
and the consequences of pressure in closed systems. Its metaphorical power
comes from the invisibility principle: good plumbing disappears, and the
work is appreciated only in its absence.
