The organizational discipline of capturing, structuring, sharing, and
maintaining knowledge. Distinct from learning (which is individual) in
its focus on knowledge as an organizational asset that can be stored,
retrieved, and transmitted. Powers metaphors about information overload,
institutional memory, and knowledge as territory.
