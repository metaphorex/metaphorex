The study of formal systems, their expressive power, and their inherent
limitations. Mathematical logic provides the vocabulary for reasoning about
what can be proved, what can be computed, and where formal methods necessarily
break down. As a source frame, it supplies structural insights about the
boundaries of any rule-based system -- legal codes, software specifications,
organizational policies -- and the recurring discovery that sufficiently
powerful frameworks contain truths they cannot derive from their own rules.
