Theatrical or staged presentation before an audience -- acting, playing
roles, following scripts, entering and exiting stages. As a source domain,
performance structures how we understand social life (playing a role,
behind the scenes), authenticity (acting vs. being real), and life stages
(acts and scenes). Shakespeare's "All the world's a stage" is the canonical
expression of this mapping.
