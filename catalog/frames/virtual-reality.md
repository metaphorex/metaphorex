Computer-generated environments that users experience as places they
inhabit rather than screens they look at. As a target domain, virtual
reality is almost entirely structured by spatial metaphors borrowed from
the physical world: rooms, doors, landscapes, distances, bodies. The
frame foregrounds the tension between simulation and reality, between
presence and absence, and between the promise of unlimited spatial freedom
and the constraints of the hardware and networks that deliver it. The
concept of a shared persistent virtual world -- the metaverse -- extends
the frame from individual experience to collective inhabitation.
