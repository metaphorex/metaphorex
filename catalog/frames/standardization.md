The process of establishing uniform specifications to ensure compatibility
and interchangeability. As a target domain, standardization borrows from
manufacturing (interchangeable parts), measurement (calibration, tolerance),
and language (grammar, convention). The domain encodes the trade-off between
the efficiency gains of uniformity and the innovation costs of constraining
variation.
