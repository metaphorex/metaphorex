The domain of unrealized capacity standing at the edge of actualization:
systems, people, and ideas that have not yet been tested by contact with
reality. Covers the structural dynamics of the threshold moment -- what
exists before the first real encounter, how latent potential differs from
demonstrated capability, and the irreversible transformation that first
contact produces. Distinct from creative-process (which covers the act of
making) and life-course (which covers the full arc) -- potential-and-emergence
focuses specifically on the pre-contact state and the crossing point.
