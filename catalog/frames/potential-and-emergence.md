The domain of unrealized capacity on the verge of becoming actual.
Encompasses seeds, beginnings, gestation, first steps, and the paradox of
the new thing that is simultaneously fragile and full of possibility.
Productive as a target domain for archetypes (Divine Child, Maiden) and for
organizational metaphors around greenfield projects, startup energy, and
untested systems approaching their first real-world encounter.
