The domain of unrealized capacity becoming actual. Seeds, embryos, sprouts,
and new beginnings share a structural pattern: something small and vulnerable
carries disproportionate future significance. The frame captures both the
fragility of early stages (exposure, dependency, threat of premature death)
and the paradoxical resilience of things that survive their vulnerable period
to become something far larger than their origins suggested.
