The study of Earth's physical structure, substances, history, and the processes
that shape them. As a source domain, geology provides metaphors for deep time,
gradual transformation, hidden structural forces, and the distinction between
surface features and underlying dynamics. Geological processes operate on
timescales that dwarf human perception, making the frame structurally productive
for reasoning about slow change that becomes visible only in retrospect:
erosion, sedimentation, tectonic drift. The frame also encodes the insight that
landscapes are shaped more by what is removed than by what is added.
