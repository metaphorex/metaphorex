The contractual transfer of risk from a policyholder to an insurer in exchange
for regular premium payments. Insurance operates by pooling many individual
risks into a collective that is statistically predictable even when individual
outcomes are not. As a source domain, insurance provides metaphors for any
arrangement where a certain small cost is exchanged for protection against an
uncertain large loss -- hedging strategies, safety margins, redundant systems,
and precautionary policies.
