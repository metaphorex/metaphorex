The sense of self derived from one's work, expertise, and professional
community membership. As a target domain, professional identity borrows
from citizenship (belonging, membership), performance (role, mask),
and territory (claiming a place, defending turf). The domain foregrounds
the tension between felt competence and external validation, and between
authentic self-expression and role conformity.
