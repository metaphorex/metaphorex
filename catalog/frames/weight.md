The felt heaviness or lightness of physical objects and the effort required
to bear them. As a source domain, weight maps onto difficulty (heavy
workload, light duties), importance (weighty matters, gravitas),
responsibility (burden of proof, shouldering the load), and emotion (heavy
heart, light spirits). One of the most deeply embodied source frames --
humans gauge weight from infancy and the mapping is near-universal.
