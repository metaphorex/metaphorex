The felt experience of heaviness and lightness -- objects that resist lifting,
loads that press down on the body, the gravitational pull that anchors
everything to the ground. Weight is one of the earliest physical experiences:
infants learn that some things are hard to move before they can speak. As a
source domain, weight maps effortlessly onto difficulty, importance, seriousness,
and emotional burden. Heavy things demand effort, attention, and respect; light
things are easy, trivial, dismissible.
