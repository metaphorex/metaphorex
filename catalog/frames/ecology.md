The study of relationships between organisms and their environments. As a
source domain, ecology provides a rich vocabulary for systems that are
interconnected, self-regulating, and vulnerable to cascading failure.
The frame foregrounds interdependence over independence, dynamic equilibrium
over static balance, and the idea that removing one element can collapse
an entire network. Its metaphorical power comes from the intuitive appeal
of "nature knows best" -- but ecologists themselves know that nature is
indifferent, not wise.
