The scientific study of mind and behavior. As a source domain for metaphor,
psychology supplies models of cognitive bias, self-deception, motivation, and
assessment. Its metaphorical exports are distinctive because they name the
systematic ways human judgment departs from accuracy -- blind spots, biases,
and heuristics that shape perception before conscious reasoning begins. The
frame foregrounds the gap between how people believe they think and how they
actually think.
