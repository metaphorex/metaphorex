The accumulated knowledge and judgment that comes from prolonged engagement
with a domain. As a target domain, experience borrows from material science
(seasoning, tempering, hardening), agriculture (ripening, harvesting), and
navigation (knowing the terrain). The domain encodes the distinction between
knowledge that can be transmitted verbally and knowledge that can only be
acquired through direct encounter.
