Physical locomotion through space -- walking, running, flying, crawling,
stopping. Distinguished from journeys (which emphasize purposeful travel
with origin and destination) by its focus on the kinesthetic experience of
motion itself: speed, direction, fluidity, and impediment. As a source
domain, movement maps onto time (time flies), cognition (quick-witted),
progress (moving forward), and social change (social movement).
