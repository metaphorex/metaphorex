Physical motion through space -- walking, running, leaping, stumbling. Where
the travel frame emphasizes origin-to-destination structure, movement focuses
on the kinetic qualities: speed, agility, grace, clumsiness. As a source
domain it maps naturally onto cognitive and social processes where pace and
fluidity matter -- quick thinking, nimble reasoning, sluggish bureaucracy.
The embodied experience of moving one's own body gives this frame its
intuitive force.
