The practice of managing relations between parties through protocol,
convention, and mutual accommodation. As a source domain for metaphor,
diplomacy supplies models of tolerance (accepting what you would not send),
protocol (agreed-upon forms that enable communication), and the tension
between strictness and flexibility in exchanges. Its metaphorical exports
emphasize that interoperability requires each party to absorb some cost of
the other party's imperfection.
