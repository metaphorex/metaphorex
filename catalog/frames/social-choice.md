The study of how individual preferences are aggregated into collective
decisions. As a target domain, social choice absorbs impossibility results
and trade-off structures from mathematical logic. The frame foregrounds the
gap between individual rationality and collective coherence: what seems
reasonable for each person may be impossible for the group. Its metaphorical
power lies in formalizing the intuition that no system of collective decision
can be simultaneously fair, consistent, and non-dictatorial.
