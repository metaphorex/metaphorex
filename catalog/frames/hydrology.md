The behavior of water bodies -- inflow, outflow, evaporation, and the
concentration or dilution of dissolved substances. Closed or terminal
basins like the Dead Sea, where inflow evaporates rather than draining,
produce increasing salinity over time. This dynamic maps onto any system
where the outflow is selective: the volatile elements leave, the residue
accumulates, and the remaining mixture grows increasingly concentrated.
