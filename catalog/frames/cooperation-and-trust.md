The dynamics of mutual reliance between agents who could benefit from betrayal.
Encompasses trust-building, reputation systems, reciprocal altruism, and the
free-rider problem. Structurally interesting because cooperation requires each
party to accept vulnerability, and the stability of cooperation depends on
mechanisms (iteration, reputation, punishment) that make defection costly.
