Discourse that examines the social, ethical, and structural consequences
of technological development. As a target domain, technology criticism
borrows heavily from apocalyptic, medical, and ecological metaphors to
frame technology as simultaneously transformative and dangerous. The
frame foregrounds the tension between techno-optimism and techno-pessimism,
the distribution of costs and benefits from technological change, and the
difficulty of governing systems whose consequences outpace deliberation.
