The narrative framework of mystery and crime fiction, where a detective
reconstructs past events from fragmentary evidence. The frame structures
inquiry as a contest between the detective's reasoning and the criminal's
concealment, with clues and red herrings as the primary currency.
Transferred to any domain where truth must be reconstructed from
incomplete or deliberately obscured evidence.
