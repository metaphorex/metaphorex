The craft of selecting, ordering, and joining shots into a coherent film. As a
source domain, editing foregrounds the invisible art of omission -- what to cut
matters more than what to keep. The frame encodes the tension between continuity
(seamless flow) and discontinuity (deliberate rupture), the hierarchy of
criteria that govern each decision, and the paradox that the best editing is
the editing you do not notice. Its metaphorical power comes from the moment of
the cut: a binary decision (here, not there) that cascades into meaning.
