The experience of tasks and situations that resist easy completion. As a
target domain, difficulty is understood through weight (heavy burden),
terrain (uphill battle, rough road), resistance (pushing through), and
physical confinement (stuck, trapped). The frame is grounded in bodily
experience: difficulty is felt as physical exertion.
