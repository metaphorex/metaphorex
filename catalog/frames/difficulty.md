The abstract experience of tasks that resist completion -- problems that demand
effort, challenges that exceed capacity, obstacles that block progress. Difficulty
has no inherent sensory signature, which is why it borrows so heavily from
physical source domains: weight, friction, uphill movement, tangled objects.
As a target domain, difficulty is structured almost entirely by metaphor.
