The physics of light, mirrors, lenses, and visual perception. As a source
domain, optics provides metaphors for self-recognition, accurate and distorted
representation, and the conditions under which seeing becomes possible. The
frame encodes the structural insight that reflection requires a surface -- one
cannot see oneself without an external medium -- and that the quality of the
mirror determines the fidelity of the image. Productive for reasoning about
feedback, self-awareness, and the difference between what is seen and what
is there.
