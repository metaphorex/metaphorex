The domain of managing a household as a bounded social space -- receiving
guests, screening visitors, controlling entry. As a source frame, household
management provides a model for any system that must distinguish legitimate
from illegitimate inputs before admitting them: the householder has authority,
the doorkeeper has judgment, and the threshold is the point of decision. The
frame is structurally richer than simple access control because it includes
social obligations (hospitality) alongside security concerns.
