Cognitive ability and mental acuity -- how smart, sharp, or quick someone
is. As a target domain, intelligence draws on speed (quick-witted),
sharpness (sharp mind, keen insight), light (brilliant, bright), and
physical agility (nimble mind, mental gymnastics). Distinguished from
intellectual-inquiry (which is about the process of thinking) by its
focus on the capacity itself.
