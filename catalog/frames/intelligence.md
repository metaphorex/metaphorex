The cognitive capacity for learning, reasoning, and problem-solving. As a
target domain, intelligence is structured by multiple source frames: light
(brilliant, dim), speed (quick, slow), sight (perceptive, blind), and
sharpness (keen, dull). The diversity of source domains reflects the
difficulty of describing cognitive ability directly -- we can only approach
it through physical analogies rooted in bodily experience.
