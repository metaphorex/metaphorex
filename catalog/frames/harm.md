The infliction or experience of damage, injury, or hindrance. As a target
domain, harm draws on sharp objects (cutting remarks), plants (thorns,
weeds), animals (stinging, biting), and physical impact (crushing blow).
The frame covers both deliberate injury and incidental nuisance, unified
by the experience of unwanted negative effect.
