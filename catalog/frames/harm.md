The domain of injury, damage, and suffering -- physical or abstract. Harm
covers everything from bodily wounds to emotional hurt to institutional
damage. As a target domain, it draws on physical source frames (cutting,
burning, striking, piercing) to make abstract suffering concrete and
communicable. The metaphorical structure of harm shapes legal concepts
(damages, injury, liability) and moral reasoning (transgression,
violation, wound).
