The domain of constructing and delivering stories across media -- oral
tradition, literature, film, television, and interactive narrative. As a
target domain, narrative and storytelling receives metaphors from
construction (building a story), plumbing (laying pipe), weaving (plot
threads), and journey (story arc). Structurally productive because it
encodes the tension between the audience's experience of unfolding events
and the author's reverse-engineered architecture of those events.
