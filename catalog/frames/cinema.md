The art and industry of motion pictures. Cinema creates immersive
constructed realities that audiences experience as compelling even while
knowing they are artificial. As a source domain, cinema supplies metaphors
of revelation (the camera pull-back, the twist ending), constructed reality
(sets, special effects, the fourth wall), and choice under dramatic
pressure (the hero's decision). Film scenes and characters become cultural
shorthand for abstract experiences -- taking the red pill, going to the
dark side, jumping the shark -- because cinema's narrative compression
makes complex ideas viscerally immediate.
