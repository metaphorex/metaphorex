Staged presentation before an audience, with the structural split between
what is shown (onstage) and what is hidden (backstage). As a source domain,
theater foregrounds the distinction between appearance and reality, the
deliberate construction of a presented self, and the audience's role in
completing the meaning. Structurally productive because it encodes the idea
that what is visible is curated, that every presentation conceals a
preparation process, and that roles can be inhabited without being identical
to the person performing them.
