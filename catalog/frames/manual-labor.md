Physical work performed on resistant material through sustained effort and
repetition. As a source domain, manual labor foregrounds the gap between knowing
what to do and having done the work, the necessity of repeated application to
overcome material resistance, and the distinction between the worker's intention
and the material's stubbornness. Structurally productive because it encodes the
insight that transformation requires labor proportional to resistance, not just
knowledge of the desired outcome.
