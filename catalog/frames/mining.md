The extraction of minerals and ores from underground or open-pit
workings. As a source domain, mining imports a structure of descent into
hidden depths, extraction of valuable material from worthless matrix, and
ever-present danger of collapse, flooding, or toxic atmosphere. The frame
carries assumptions about risk proportional to depth, the invisibility of
underground conditions from the surface, and the need for early-warning
systems. Mining metaphors pervade knowledge work (data mining, gold mine,
deep dive, canary in a coal mine) precisely because the felt experience
of searching for something valuable in dangerous, opaque conditions
transfers broadly.
