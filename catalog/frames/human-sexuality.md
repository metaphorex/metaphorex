The domain of human sexual behavior, physiology, and social norms surrounding
it. Notable as a source frame precisely because its social charge is so high
that structural borrowing from it is always conspicuous -- except when the
borrower is deep enough in abstraction to forget. The mechanics of the domain
(rhythm, coupling, friction, climax) are structurally rich but rarely exploited
in formal mapping because the taboo cost of invoking them outweighs the
structural payoff in most professional contexts.
