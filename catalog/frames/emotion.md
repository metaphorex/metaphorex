The felt experience of affective states -- joy, anger, fear, sadness,
love, disgust. As a target domain, emotion is one of the most metaphorized
areas of human experience. We understand emotions through containers (full
of joy), forces (overcome with grief), temperature (burning anger, cold
fear), weather (stormy mood), and fluids (overflowing with emotion). The
abstractness of inner feeling demands concrete metaphorical grounding.
