The domain of felt experience -- joy, anger, fear, sadness, love, disgust,
and their variants. As a target domain, emotion is structured by a wide
range of source frames: containers (emotions fill you up), forces (they
push you around), weather (they pass over you), heat (they make you boil).
The mapping direction is almost always from physical to emotional, reflecting
the difficulty of describing subjective states without concrete analogy.
