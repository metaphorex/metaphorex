The mathematics of finding the best solution from a set of alternatives
subject to constraints. As a source domain, optimization supplies concepts
of search landscapes (hills, valleys, plateaus), trade-offs (Pareto
frontiers), and the fundamental tension between exploration and exploitation.
When we say we are "optimizing for" something, searching for the "best fit,"
or "stuck in a local maximum," we are borrowing from this frame. Its
metaphorical power comes from making the structure of choice legible as
landscape navigation; its danger lies in assuming that all problems have
well-defined objective functions and that "optimal" is meaningful when
applied to domains where the evaluation criteria are contested or shifting.
