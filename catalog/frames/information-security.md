The protection of information assets through controlling access, detecting
intrusion, and managing trust boundaries. As a frame, information security
borrows heavily from military strategy (defense in depth, perimeter, attack
surface) and architecture (firewalls, vaults, zones). Its core structural
insight is that security is a gradient, not a binary: the question is never
whether a system is "secure" but what level of access is granted under what
conditions and with what verification. The frame foregrounds the adversarial
relationship between those who authorize access and those who seek
unauthorized access.
