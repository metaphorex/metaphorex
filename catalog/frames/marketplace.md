A decentralized space where many independent sellers offer goods to many
independent buyers, with no central authority controlling what is sold or at
what price. Quality and value emerge from competition, choice, and reputation
rather than from planning. The frame carries noise, redundancy, and
apparent disorder as structural features rather than defects -- the babble
of competing voices is the mechanism by which information propagates and
selection occurs.
