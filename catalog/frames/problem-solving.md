The general cognitive activity of finding solutions to difficult or
complex issues. As a source domain, problem-solving foregrounds the
existence of a goal state, obstacles between the current state and
the goal, and strategies for navigating those obstacles. The frame
imports spatial metaphors (searching a solution space, exploring
possibilities) and mechanical ones (levers, tools, mechanisms).
