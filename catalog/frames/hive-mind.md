A superorganism composed of many individuals that share a single
consciousness or purpose, sacrificing individual autonomy for collective
coordination. Drawn from eusocial insects (bees, ants, termites) and
amplified by science fiction (the Borg, the Zerg, the Tyranids), the hive
mind frame structures thinking about any system where individual agency is
absorbed into group identity. As a source domain, it imports the tension
between efficiency (the hive acts as one) and horror (the individual
ceases to exist).
