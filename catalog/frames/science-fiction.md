The literary and cinematic genre that imagines the consequences of
scientific and technological change. Science fiction serves as a uniquely
productive source domain for metaphor because it provides narrative
test-cases for technologies before they exist. When a speculative concept
(robots, cyberspace, the metaverse) migrates from fiction into engineering
and public discourse, it carries the genre's narrative assumptions: that
technology is destiny, that creation threatens its creator, and that the
future is a place we travel to rather than one we build. Science fiction
metaphors are especially potent because they arrive pre-loaded with plot --
invoking Skynet imports not just a technology but an entire cautionary
narrative.
