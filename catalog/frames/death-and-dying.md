The cessation of life and the transition surrounding it. One of the most
heavily metaphorized domains in any language -- death is almost never spoken
of directly. As a target domain, it draws on journeys (passing away),
sleep (eternal rest), harvest (the grim reaper), and darkness. The
euphemistic pressure on this domain makes it a rich site for studying
how metaphor does cultural work.
