The capacity to act despite fear -- standing firm, holding ground, facing
danger. As a target domain, courage draws extensively on physical strength
(fortitude, stout-hearted) and spatial metaphors (standing one's ground,
not backing down). The frame encodes the bodily experience of confronting
threat: muscles tense, posture stiffens, the body either holds or breaks.
