The process by which organizations lose capacity, coherence, or viability
over time. As a target domain, organizational decline draws heavily on
biological metaphors (disease, bleeding, decay) and physical metaphors
(erosion, structural failure, entropy). The frame centers on the
relationship between accumulated stressors and the system's capacity to
absorb them.
