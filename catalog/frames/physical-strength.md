The bodily capacity to exert force, bear weight, and resist pressure.
As a source domain, physical strength maps onto moral character (strong
character, pillar of strength), emotional resilience (holding it together),
arguments (strong case, weak argument), and institutional stability
(strong economy). The opposite -- weakness, fragility, collapse -- is
equally productive as a source for failure and vulnerability.
