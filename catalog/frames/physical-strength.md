The capacity of a body to exert force, bear weight, and resist external
pressure. As a source domain, physical strength maps onto moral, emotional,
and institutional qualities -- strong leadership, weak arguments, the backbone
of an organization. The binary structure (strong vs. weak) and the embodied
experience of muscular effort make this one of the most pervasive and least
noticed metaphorical source domains in everyday language.
