The practice of producing approximate values when exact computation is
infeasible or unnecessary. As a source domain, estimation foregrounds the gap
between precision and accuracy: an estimate can be precise (many decimal places)
without being accurate (close to the true value), and vice versa. Structurally
productive because it makes the cost of measurement explicit, distinguishes
between systematic and random error, and encodes the insight that all
prediction is estimation under uncertainty.
