The arrangement and maintenance of physical and digital work environments
for efficiency and safety. As a target domain, workplace organization
borrows from domestic tidiness (a place for everything), military
discipline (inspection-ready), and ecology (habitat design). The domain
foregrounds the tension between individual preference and collective
standardization, and between one-time cleanup and ongoing discipline.
