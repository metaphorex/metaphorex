The dynamic between a pursuer who wants something and a quarry who resists
giving it. As a source domain, pursuit-and-escape provides a minimal model
of conflict driven by asymmetric desire: one party advances, the other
retreats, and the outcome depends on persistence, strategy, and terrain.
Structurally useful because it reduces complex negotiations to their
motivational skeleton -- who wants what, who resists, and what changes when
one side stops running.
