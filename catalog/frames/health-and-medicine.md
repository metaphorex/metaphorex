The practice of diagnosing, treating, and preventing disease in living
organisms. As a source domain, health and medicine exports metaphors of
pathology (sick organizations, toxic cultures), diagnosis (root cause
analysis), contagion (viral ideas, spreading panic), and cure (silver
bullets, remedies). Its metaphorical power rests on the universal
experience of bodily vulnerability and the authority granted to those
who can name what is wrong and prescribe a fix.
