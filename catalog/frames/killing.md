The deliberate ending of life -- slaughter, butchery, hunting, execution.
As a source domain, killing vocabulary transfers into commerce (make a killing),
comedy (killed it), competition (crushed them), and criticism (savage review,
eviscerating takedown). The visceral violence of the frame lends intensity
to domains where the actual stakes are financial or reputational rather than
mortal. The slaughterhouse and abattoir provide a particularly rich sub-frame
for industrial-scale destruction applied metaphorically to market competition.
