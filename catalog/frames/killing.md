The act of causing death, especially through violence. As a source domain,
killing vocabulary transfers into commerce (make a killing, slaughter the
competition), performance (killed it on stage), humor (that joke killed),
and exhaustion (it's killing me). The extreme finality of death lends
hyperbolic force to these transfers.
