The mechanics of trust, reputation, and verification between agents in
a social system. Distinct from general social behavior in its focus on
the epistemic problem: how do you know who to trust, and what do you do
when you cannot know? This frame powers security metaphors like
zero-trust, where interpersonal paranoia becomes architectural principle.
