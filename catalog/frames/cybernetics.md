The science of communication and control in animals and machines, founded
by Norbert Wiener in 1948. Cybernetics studies how systems regulate
themselves through feedback -- sensors detect deviation from a goal, and
controllers adjust behavior to reduce the error. As a source domain,
cybernetics provides the vocabulary of feedback loops, set-points, error
signals, and adaptive control that has been mapped onto everything from
organizational management to psychotherapy. The frame's power lies in its
abstraction: the same feedback diagram describes a thermostat, an immune
response, and a performance review process.
