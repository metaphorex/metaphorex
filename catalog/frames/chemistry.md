The science of substances, their properties, and transformations. As a source
domain, chemistry provides metaphors for controlled transformation, measured
dosage, reactions that require precise conditions, and the distinction between
stable and volatile states. The frame's structural productivity comes from its
vocabulary of gradual change under controlled conditions -- titration, dilution,
catalysis -- and the insight that the same substance at different concentrations
can be medicine or poison.
