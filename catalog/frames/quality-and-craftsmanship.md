The domain of what makes work good, durable, and fit for purpose. As a target
domain, quality and craftsmanship receives metaphors from every making tradition
-- carpentry, cooking, metalwork, software. Structurally productive because it
encodes the tension between measurable standards (tolerances, specs, tests) and
the irreducible role of skilled judgment that no specification can fully capture.
The domain forces a reckoning with the question of whether quality is a property
of the object or a relationship between object, maker, and context.
