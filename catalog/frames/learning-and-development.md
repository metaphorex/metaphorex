The process of acquiring skills and knowledge through instruction, practice,
and experience. As a target domain, learning and development receives
metaphors from construction (building knowledge), journeys (learning path),
agriculture (cultivating talent), and performance arts (rehearsal, practice).
The domain foregrounds the gap between knowing and doing, the role of
failure in improvement, and the distinction between safe practice
environments and high-stakes performance.
