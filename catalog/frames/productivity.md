The measure and optimization of output relative to input in work
contexts. As a source domain, productivity foregrounds throughput,
efficiency, and the elimination of waste. The frame draws heavily
from manufacturing metaphors (pipeline, bottleneck, throughput) and
increasingly from culinary ones (mise en place, flow state).
