The cognitive and institutional management of uncertain outcomes --
estimating probabilities, distributing exposure, hedging against loss,
and deciding under incomplete information. As a target domain, risk
absorbs metaphors from gambling (betting, stakes), agriculture (eggs
in baskets), navigation (charting a course), and warfare (defensive
positions). The domain spans informal folk wisdom through formal
decision theory and financial engineering.
