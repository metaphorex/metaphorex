The mathematical study of strategic interaction between rational agents.
Encompasses zero-sum and non-zero-sum games, iterated and one-shot encounters,
and the tension between individual rationality and collective welfare. As a
source domain, it provides the vocabulary of Nash equilibria, dominant
strategies, and payoff matrices that has colonized economics, political science,
evolutionary biology, and AI alignment discourse.
