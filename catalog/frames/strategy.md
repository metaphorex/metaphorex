The art of aligning means with ends under conditions of uncertainty and
opposition. Strategy as a frame foregrounds the relationship between
available resources, desired objectives, and the actions of adversaries or
competitors. It imports the temporal dimension -- sequencing, tempo, timing --
as a first-class concern, distinguishing it from planning (which assumes a
stable environment) and tactics (which address immediate situations without
reference to larger objectives).
