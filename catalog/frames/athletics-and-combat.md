The domain of physical contest -- wrestling, ball games, boxing, pankration,
and other competitive athletic activities. As a source frame, athletics and
combat supply a model for situations requiring reactive readiness, embodied
skill, and engagement with an unpredictable adversary. The frame distinguishes
between the athlete's preparation (training, conditioning) and the contest
itself (improvisation, adaptation). Ancient sources frequently drew on this
frame because athletic competition was a central cultural institution and its
structural dynamics were universally understood.
