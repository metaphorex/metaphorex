The conceptualization of moral life through bookkeeping metaphors --
debts owed, credits earned, balances maintained, and accounts settled.
Moral accounting treats good and bad acts as entries in a ledger,
where justice means the books eventually balance. As a target domain
it absorbs metaphors from agriculture (reaping what you sow), commerce
(paying your dues), and law (settling accounts).
