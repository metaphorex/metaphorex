Mechanisms designed to prevent harm by detecting dangerous conditions and
activating protective responses. As a source domain, safety systems provide
vocabulary for software reliability patterns: watchdogs, circuit breakers,
health checks, dead man's switches. The frame centers on the relationship
between monitoring, detection, and automated response -- the assumption
that safety requires active vigilance rather than passive protection.
