Traditional stories transmitted orally across generations, encoding practical
wisdom about social cooperation, deception, resource scarcity, and power.
Unlike mythology (which deals with sacred origins and cosmic order), folklore
operates at the human scale -- its protagonists are peasants, travelers, and
animals, not gods. As a source domain, folklore provides narrative structures
where cleverness defeats strength, small actions compound into large outcomes,
and apparent foolishness conceals deeper wisdom.
