Structured physical combat disciplines that encode knowledge in repeatable
bodily forms (kata), master-student transmission (sensei/kohai), and
deliberate practice in dedicated spaces (dojo). Martial arts vocabulary
migrates into management and software through the lean manufacturing
tradition, which borrowed heavily from judo and aikido philosophy -- the
idea that skill is built through disciplined repetition of fundamental
movements, not through raw force or improvisation.
