The domain of knowing, believing, and questioning what is real. Encompasses
the relationship between appearance and reality, the grounds for justified
belief, the nature of deception and self-deception, and the question of
whether one's entire framework of understanding might be mistaken. As a
target domain, epistemology is pervasive but abstract -- everyone navigates
the gap between what seems true and what is true, but the experience is
difficult to articulate without spatial and sensory metaphors (seeing the
truth, being in the dark, waking up, going deeper). Science fiction and
philosophy both supply frames for this domain: caves, matrices, veils,
mirrors, simulations.
