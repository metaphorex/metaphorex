The activity of organizing resources and establishing conditions before
execution begins. As a target domain, planning and preparation receives
metaphors from crafts, military strategy, cooking, and athletics -- all
fields where the quality of preparation determines the outcome of
performance. The domain foregrounds the tension between preparation as
productive investment and preparation as procrastination.
