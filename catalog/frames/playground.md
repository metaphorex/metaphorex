Bounded spaces designed for consequence-free exploration -- sandboxes,
playgrounds, play areas where children can build, destroy, and experiment
without real-world consequences. As a source domain, the playground
foregrounds safety through containment: walls keep the danger out and the
mess in, supervision is optional but available, and nothing built inside
needs to survive past the session.
