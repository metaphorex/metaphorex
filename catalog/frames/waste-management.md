Municipal sanitation infrastructure: the systems that collect, transport,
and dispose of refuse. Garbage trucks run on schedules, bins overflow when
not emptied, landfills fill up, and recycling reclaims useful material from
discarded objects. The frame carries an out-of-sight-out-of-mind quality --
the whole point of waste management is that someone else handles it so you
don't have to think about it.
