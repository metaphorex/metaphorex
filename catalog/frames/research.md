The systematic investigation of a domain to establish facts, develop
theories, or reach new conclusions. Distinguished from casual inquiry
by its methodological self-awareness and from engineering by its
orientation toward understanding rather than production. Powers
metaphors about knowledge landscapes, frontiers, and exploration.
