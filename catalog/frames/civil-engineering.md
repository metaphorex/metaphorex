The design and construction of infrastructure that spans, supports, and
connects across physical terrain -- bridges, tunnels, dams, roads. As a
source domain, civil engineering foregrounds the tension between two sides
that must be connected without collapsing the independence of either. The
bridge is the emblematic structure: it must bear load while spanning a gap,
and its design is constrained by the terrain on both sides.
