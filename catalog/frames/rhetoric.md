The art and study of persuasion through language. As a source domain,
rhetoric exports structural concepts like kairos (opportune timing),
ethos/pathos/logos (modes of appeal), and the distinction between
argument and audience. The frame foregrounds that communication is
never neutral: every utterance has a speaker, an audience, an occasion,
and a strategic relationship among the three.
