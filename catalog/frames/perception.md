The process of becoming aware of reality through the senses or through
cognition. As a target domain, perception is structured by the opposition
between seeing and blindness, between surface appearances and underlying
truth. Metaphors targeting perception often involve a moment of rupture --
scales falling from the eyes, a veil being lifted, an awakening from
sleep -- in which the perceiver transitions from false to true awareness.
The frame carries epistemological weight: what counts as real, who gets to
see it, and what it costs to look.
