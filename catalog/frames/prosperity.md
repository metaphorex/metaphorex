The state of thriving, flourishing, and enjoying abundance. As a target
domain, prosperity draws heavily on plant growth (flourishing, blooming,
bearing fruit), upward movement (rising, climbing), and health (robust,
vigorous). As a source domain, prosperity vocabulary lends positive
evaluation to non-economic contexts: a "rich" inner life, a "prosperous"
intellectual tradition.
