The domain of material and social success -- wealth, abundance, thriving,
and their opposites. As a target domain, prosperity borrows structure from
plant growth (flourishing, bearing fruit), upward motion (rising fortunes),
and health (economic vitality). The domain covers both individual and
collective welfare, from personal financial success to national economic
conditions.
