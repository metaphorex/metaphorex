What we wear and how we wear it. As a source domain, clothing foregrounds
identity performance (you are what you wear), the tension between protection
and display, and the social grammar of appropriateness. Its metaphorical
transfers are strongest when the target involves presentation, layering,
or the visible expression of invisible commitments. The frame provides
natural vocabulary for "putting on" roles, "wearing" responsibilities,
and "outgrowing" positions.
