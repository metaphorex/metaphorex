The structured progression from novice to expert within a professional
domain. As a target domain, professional development borrows from craft
traditions (apprenticeship, journeyman, master), agriculture (cultivation,
seasoning), and construction (building, foundation). The domain foregrounds
the tension between formal credentials and demonstrated competence, and
between time-served and skill-acquired models of expertise.
