Atmospheric conditions and their changes -- storms, sunshine, fog, wind,
pressure systems. Distinguished from climate (long-term patterns) by its
immediacy and unpredictability. As a source domain, weather maps onto any
experience that arrives unbidden, varies in intensity, and passes without
the experiencer's control. The key structural feature is that weather
happens to you; you do not choose it.
