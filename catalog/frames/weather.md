Atmospheric conditions and their changes -- storms, sunshine, clouds,
wind, rain. As a source domain, weather maps powerfully onto emotion
(stormy mood, sunny disposition, clouded judgment), social situations
(political climate, storm of controversy), and unpredictability (weather
the storm, fair-weather friend). The mapping emphasizes that weather, like
emotion, arrives unbidden, varies in intensity, and eventually passes.
