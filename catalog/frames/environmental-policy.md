The governance of human interactions with ecological systems through
regulation, conservation mandates, and resource management. As a target
domain, environmental policy inherits framing from ecology (ecosystems,
balance, resilience) and from governance (jurisdiction, compliance,
enforcement). The field's vocabulary reveals which ecological paradigms
have colonized policy thinking -- "restoring balance" encodes
equilibrium assumptions, while "adaptive management" encodes resilience
thinking.
