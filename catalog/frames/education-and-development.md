The domain of intentional human development -- teaching, training, mentoring,
and the acquisition of capabilities over time. As a target domain, education
and development draws heavily on agricultural metaphors (cultivating minds,
planting seeds, harvesting results) and construction metaphors (building
knowledge, laying foundations). The frame foregrounds the gap between
instruction and learning, the role of the learner's own agency, and the
long time horizons over which development becomes visible.
