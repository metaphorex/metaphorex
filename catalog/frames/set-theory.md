The mathematical study of collections of objects and the relationships between
them. As a source domain, set theory provides the foundational vocabulary for
reasoning about membership, containment, size, and infinity. Its metaphorical
power comes from making abstract mathematical properties tangible through
concrete scenarios (hotels, libraries, catalogues), but its most important
contribution is the demonstration that human intuition systematically fails
when applied to infinite collections.
