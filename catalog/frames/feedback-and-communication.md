The exchange of information intended to modify behavior, understanding, or
performance. As a target domain, feedback absorbs metaphors from navigation
(course correction), warfare (targeting), medicine (diagnosis), and the arts
(direction, coaching). The frame encodes the asymmetry between the giver's
intent and the receiver's interpretation, the distinction between evaluative
feedback (judgment) and actionable feedback (instruction), and the temporal
gap between signal and response.
