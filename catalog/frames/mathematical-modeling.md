The practice of representing real-world phenomena as formal mathematical
structures. As a source domain, mathematical modeling foregrounds the
deliberate trade-off between accuracy and tractability -- every model is
wrong, but some are useful. The frame encodes the tensions between
elegance and fidelity, parsimony and completeness, closed-form solutions
and numerical approximation. Its metaphorical power derives from the
prestige of mathematical certainty applied to messy reality.
